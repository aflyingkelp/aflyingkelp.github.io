Welcome to Mountain of Faith

This site provides the Touhou 10 browser launcher and runtime.

Import your own game

Use Prepare your Touhou 10 game files to create an import ZIP from your own th10.dat, or import an existing EAGLER TOUHOU package. Imported game data stays on your device.

Controls

Arrow keys move, Z shoots, X uses a bomb, Shift focuses, and Escape pauses. Keyboard, gamepad and touch options are available in the launcher.
